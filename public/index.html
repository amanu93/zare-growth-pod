Onboarding in three weeks

A discovery period to identify what your pipeline needs, then we integrate the pod with your stack, build your dashboard, log your baseline, and the team is live. Need another role later, in sales or beyond? We vet and place it on your team.

A touch is one contact attempt with one prospect: a cold or follow-up email, a LinkedIn request or message, a call or a voicemail. A prospect in a five-step sequence counts as five touches, so 500 touches a week is roughly 100 to 125 prospects being worked at any one time. Every touch is counted on your dashboard.

How we measure it

A baseline during onboarding, then meetings and engagement checked at month 6, with founder time and recruitment measured the same way. Your strategy manager tallies it; you see it on the dashboard and in the monthly review.

Meetings

Count when booked and held, with "qualified" defined at kickoff. Together with weekly touches, this is what we are held to.

Founder time

During onboarding you log one typical week of outreach, scheduling, content, proposals, CRM and onboarding work. At month 6 you log it again, minus the time you spend directing the pod. Closing calls don't count: a working pod adds those.

Recruitment

Your last two or three hires set the baseline: days from job description to signed offer, and cost per hire including ads, agency fees and interview hours. We measure the pod the same way.
